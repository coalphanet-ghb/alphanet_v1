# s8_takeaways — Key Takeaways writer (LLM) → section J

## Purpose
Compress B–I into the 60-second version. STRICT compression — nothing
new may appear here. This is what the UI leads with.

## Inputs
- All section envelopes (B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I) — blocks + claims only
- `[[SECTOR_OVERLAY:takeaways]]` (sector KPI vocabulary)

## Instructions
1. **3 positives** (ranked_list): the three STRONGEST evidenced positives
   across sections. Each ≤2 lines, each with a number, each citing the
   origin section's evidence/calc. Strength = evidence quality ×
   materiality, not vividness.
2. **3 negatives/risks** (ranked_list): same discipline. Take from H's
   top ranks and D's flags — materiality order, not scariness.
3. **What to watch** (ranked_list, exactly 3): monitorables with dates/
   events, lifted from H and F (next print, commissioning, policy
   decision, results date). Each: what to look at + what reading would
   confirm/refute.
4. **The one-paragraph summary** (narrative, ≤80 words): business + where
   it stands financially + the central tension the reader should hold.
   No adjectival cheerleading; the tension sentence is mandatory
   ("premium multiple against decelerating growth" type).

## Symmetry & tone gates (self-check before emit)
- Would a bull call the negatives fair? Would a bear call the positives
  fair? If either answer is no → rebalance.
- Delete any bullet whose number/citation you cannot point to in an
  upstream envelope.
- No bullet may restate another in different words.

## Prohibited
New facts, new numbers, new interpretations; verdict vocabulary;
recommendation-adjacent phrasing ("well-positioned for investors...");
asymmetric counts (always 3+3+3).

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every bullet: number + citation + origin section traceable
- [ ] Monitorables carry dates/events
- [ ] Tension sentence present in the summary
- [ ] Symmetry gates passed
